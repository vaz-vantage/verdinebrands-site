This policy explains how Verdine Brands LLC (“we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through this website, verdinebrands.com.

Scope

This policy applies to verdinebrands.com only. Each online store operated by one of our brands publishes its own privacy policy, which applies to visits and purchases on that store.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you contact us by email or telephone, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number and the content of your message.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, date and time of access and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the website.

This website does not offer accounts, does not sell products and does not include contact forms.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels or similar tracking technologies. Fonts and icons are served from our own domain.

How we use information

We use personal information only to respond to your inquiry, to operate and secure this website and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate this website and our email, such as hosting and email providers, and only to the extent needed for those services, or where disclosure is required by law.

Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and to maintain our business records, and then delete it. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states, you may have additional rights under applicable law. Send requests to hello@verdinebrands.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

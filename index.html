About

A company that builds brands and runs them directly.

Verdine Brands LLC is a brand development and operating company organized in the State of Wyoming. We create consumer brands and manage them end to end, from product selection to the online store and customer service.

Every brand we operate is owned and run by Verdine Brands LLC. Customers, partners and platforms deal with one accountable legal entity, whose details are published on this website.

How we operate

From product to customer, in one company.

Each brand follows the same model. We are responsible for every step, so customers always know who stands behind the product they buy.

  1. 1
    Step 1

    Product

    We select and specify products together with our suppliers, with close attention to quality and packaging.

  2. 2
    Step 2

    Brand

    Each product line is built into its own brand, with a clear name, identity and positioning.

  3. 3
    Step 3

    Online store

    Our brands sell directly to customers through their own online stores, with prices and terms shown before checkout.

  4. 4
    Step 4

    Customer care

    Customers are supported directly by the brand’s own support team, before and after their purchase.

One accountable company

All brands are operated by Verdine Brands LLC, with our legal details published openly.

Focused ranges

Each brand concentrates on a single category and a small number of products.

Clear terms

Prices, shipping and return conditions are stated on each store before purchase.
